SnapClean Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026　|　Last updated: October 18, 2026

SnapClean ("the App") is a device cleaning and diagnostic tool. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App has no server: your files, photos and app information are never uploaded anywhere.

1. Data the App accesses

Third-party SDKs currently used: Google AdMob (advertising).

Everything is processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded or shared with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Photos and videos (local read) — after you grant the Photos and Videos permission, the App scans locally for duplicate photos, blurry photos, screenshots and burst photos to show you cleanable items. Image content never leaves your device.
  • Installed apps and cache size (optional) — only if you turn on "Usage access" in system settings does the App read other apps' cache size, to display "Other app cache". Without this permission, all other features still work.
  • Device and system info (local display) — storage, memory, battery, CPU status, shown only on your device.
  • Installation packages (local) — .apk files on your device are listed; deletion happens only after you confirm.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). For this, Google reads your device's Advertising ID to serve and measure ads.

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in system settings, or opt out of ad personalization.
  • Ad-related data handling follows the Google Privacy Policy.
  • The ad SDK does not read your photos, files, or installed app list for advertising purposes.

3. App analytics

The App currently does not integrate any third-party analytics or attribution service. Ads are served by Google AdMob and its data handling follows the Google Privacy Policy. If analytics is added in a future release, this policy will be updated first.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, SMS or call logs.
  • We do not upload your photos, videos or any file to a server.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not use Usage Access data for advertising or analytics.

5. Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO — scan duplicate, blurry and screenshot photos (Android 13+).
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Usage access) — optional, only to read other apps' cache size.
  • KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES — stop background processes when you ask.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only to request and display ads.
  • The App does not request MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES, and does not use Accessibility services.

6. Data storage and deletion

All scan results stay on your device; uninstalling the App removes everything. You can revoke any permission at any time in system settings — the related feature stops, while the rest of the App keeps working.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA) and similar regions, you may request access, correction, deletion, or object to certain processing. Because we hold no personal data on servers, uninstalling the App or revoking permissions normally achieves this; contact us below if you need help.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date on this page and notify you in the App.
